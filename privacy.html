If you email us, we process your email address, your name and any other information you include in your message, in order to answer your inquiry and to follow up on any resulting collaboration, partnership or support discussions. We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run this website and our communications, such as our website hosting provider (GitHub, Inc., USA) and our email provider, and only to the extent needed for those services.

Some of these providers may process data outside Switzerland and the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where this happens, we rely on safeguards recognized under applicable data protection law, such as the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.
